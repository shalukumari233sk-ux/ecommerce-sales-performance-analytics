# Business Insights & Recommendations

## 1. Executive Summary

The e-commerce dataset contains 20 customers, 15 products, and 40 orders.

Analysis of completed orders generated gross revenue of ₹10,88,400 and gross profit of ₹2,48,650, resulting in a gross margin of 22.85%.

The average order value was ₹36,280.

Overall order status analysis shows that 75% of orders were completed, while the remaining orders were cancelled, returned, or pending.

---

## 2. Key Business Insights

### Sales & Profitability

- Completed orders generated ₹10.88 lakh in gross revenue.
- Gross profit was ₹2.49 lakh.
- Overall gross margin was 22.85%.
- Average order value was ₹36,280.

### Order Performance

- 75% of total orders were completed.
- 10% of orders were cancelled.
- 7.5% of orders were returned.
- 7.5% of orders were pending.

The cancelled, returned, and pending orders represent an operational exception area that can be monitored for improvement opportunities.

### Product Performance

Product-level analysis was performed using units sold, revenue, and gross profit.

The analysis identified high-performing products based on revenue and profitability. These products can be prioritized for inventory planning and commercial activity.

### Category Performance

Category-level analysis was performed to compare units sold, revenue, and gross profit.

Categories generating stronger revenue and profitability should receive appropriate inventory and sales focus.

### Customer Performance

Customers were ranked based on completed-order revenue and order count.

High-value and repeat customers can be targeted for customer-retention initiatives.

### Regional Performance

State-level analysis was performed using completed orders, revenue, and gross profit.

The results can help identify stronger-performing markets and regions requiring further investigation.

### Sales Trend

Monthly revenue and gross profit were analyzed to identify changes in sales performance over time.

Based on the monthly analysis:

- January generated the highest revenue at ₹181,900.
- February revenue was ₹102,600.
- March revenue was ₹129,600.
- April revenue was ₹115,200.
- May revenue was ₹134,500.
- June revenue was ₹114,200.
- July revenue was ₹101,600.
- August revenue was ₹58,500.
- September revenue was ₹92,300.
- October generated the lowest revenue at ₹58,000.

The results show a decline in revenue toward the later months, with August and October recording comparatively lower sales.

### Payment Method

Payment methods were analyzed based on completed orders to understand customer payment preferences.

### Discount Analysis

Orders were analyzed across different discount levels to compare completion, cancellation, return, and pending outcomes.

---

## 3. Business Recommendations

### Recommendation 1 — Focus on High-Performing Products

Prioritize inventory availability and commercial activity for products generating strong revenue and gross profit.

### Recommendation 2 — Improve Operational Monitoring

Track cancelled, returned, and pending orders as exception KPIs and investigate recurring causes.

### Recommendation 3 — Strengthen Customer Retention

Identify high-value and repeat customers and consider targeted retention initiatives.

### Recommendation 4 — Review Regional Performance

Compare state-level revenue and profitability to identify markets with stronger potential and areas requiring improvement.

### Recommendation 5 — Monitor Profitability Alongside Revenue

Use gross profit and gross margin alongside revenue when evaluating products and categories.

### Recommendation 6 — Evaluate Discount Effectiveness

Compare discount levels against order completion and operational outcomes before increasing promotional intensity.

### Recommendation 7 — Investigate Monthly Sales Decline

Investigate the reasons behind lower revenue in August and October, including changes in product demand, customer activity, promotions, and product mix.
